Done — AP63203 buck converter block authored and powered up from 12V.

I've created a complete AP63203 Buck block with:

    Real chip: AP63203WU-7 in TSOT-26 package with full pinout
    Support circuitry: Input caps (C1, C2), switching inductor (L1), output caps (C3, C4), and feedback resistor divider (R1, R2) to set the 3.3V output
    Exposed terminals: vin (12V input), vout (3.3V output), en (enable), gnd (ground)

The design is wired with a barrel jack (J1) supplying 12V at up to 3A, feeding directly into the buck converter. The enable pin is tied high to the 3.3V output for automatic startup. All 8 parts are on the BOM ready for manufacture.
