Architecture

USB-C (J1) → USBC1 → CHG1 (Li-ion charger) → BAT+ → LDO1 (3.3 V)
                                                         │
                          ┌──────────────────────────────┤
                          ▼           ▼              ▼
                        NTC1        CMP1           BUZ1
                     (NTC divider) (LM393 comp) (2N7002 driver)
                          │           │              │
                       SENSE ────► IN_P  OUT ──► CTRL   BUZZ_OUT → J3

Block 	Key Parts
USB-C input 	USBC1 + J1 USB-C connector
Li-ion charger 	CHG1, battery on JST J2
3.3 V LDO 	LDO1, powers all logic
NTC sensing 	10 kΩ pull-up + 10 kΩ/B3950 NTC + 100 pF filter
Comparator 	LM393DR, 27 k/10 kΩ divider → V_REF = 0.97 V ≈ NTC at 50 °C, 10 kΩ pull-up, 1 MΩ hysteresis
Buzzer driver 	2N7002 MOSFET + 10 kΩ gate R + flyback diode
LED indicator 	Charge-status LED on CHG1.chrg
Standby Draw (manual, from 3.3 V rail)
Path 	Current
NTC divider (10 kΩ + ~4.9 kΩ NTC at 25 °C) 	~220 µA
CMP1 VREF divider (27 kΩ + 10 kΩ) 	~89 µA
LM393 quiescent (datasheet typ) 	~0.8 µA
LDO1 quiescent 	~75 µA
Total standby 	~385 µA @ 3.3 V ≈ 1.3 mW

On a typical 2000 mAh Li-ion cell that's ~218 days of standby — very comfortable for a battery alarm.
One Remaining Warning

USB inrush: USBC1 + CHG1 together hang ~32 µF on VBUS. The USB spec prefers <10 µF before a soft-start. For a simple charger-only design this is usually tolerated (and charger ICs often handle it), but if you want strict compliance, add a small-value series resistor or load switch at VBUS before the bulk caps.
