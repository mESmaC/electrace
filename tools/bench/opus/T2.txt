Signal chain: USB-C (J1) → TP4056 charger (CHG1) → Li-ion cell (J2/JST-PH) on the shared VBAT rail → NTC sensing stage (NTC1) → LM393 comparator @ 50 °C trip (CMP1) → buzzer driver (BUZ1) → piezo header (J3).

    Charging: J1 VBUS/GND → CHG1; CHG1.batt + the cell share VBAT (standard single-cell charge-and-run — flagged, reviewed, acknowledged as intended). USB-C declared at 5 V / 1.5 A, JST at 4.2 V.
    Trip point: NTC1 is a 10k/B3950 NTC with a 10k top resistor; at 50 °C it's ≈3.6 kΩ → SENSE ≈ 0.265·VBAT. CMP1's reference is R_TOP 27k / R_BOT 10k = 0.270·VBAT. SENSE falls through the reference as temperature rises, so it trips right around 50 °C, with R_HYS 1M adding hysteresis so it doesn't chatter.
    Output: comparator OUT → buzzer driver gate; the piezo + 2N7002 + flyback diode live inside BUZ1, with BUZZ_OUT brought to header J3.

Standby draw (alarm idle, not tripped, ~4.2 V cell):

On a typical 2000 mAh 18650 that's ~3+ months of standby. The two dividers (~324 µA) are the biggest knob — if you want to cut it, I can bump the NTC top resistor and comparator divider to 100k-class values to drop the resistive floor to well under 100 µA.
