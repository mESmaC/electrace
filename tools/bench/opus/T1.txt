Done. Here's what I built:

AP63203 buck block (@user/ap63203-buck-3-3v-2) — authored from the real datasheet, not a guess:

    U1 = AP63203WU-7 in TSOT-23-6 with the actual pinout (1=FB, 2=GND, 3=EN, 4=VIN, 5=SW, 6=BST).
    Support parts per the typical application: C1 22µF input bulk + C2 1µF input ceramic on VIN, C3 0.1µF bootstrap cap between BST↔SW, L1 3.3µH from SW to output, and C4 22µF output cap. Since this is the fixed-3.3V part, FB connects to the output directly — so I tied FB to VOUT internally.
    Exposed terminals: vin / vout / en / gnd.

Integration: placed it as BUCK1, added a 2.1mm barrel jack (J1) declared as 12 V @ 2 A power entry, and wired J1.+ → vin, J1.− → gnd. I tied EN to VIN so the converter auto-starts as the 12 V rail comes up (the EN pin is a high voltage pin and can be directly connected to VIN to automatically start up the device).

One open item by design: the 3.3 V vout rail is intentionally dangling — it's flagged as an open net until you connect a downstream load. The 12 V input range is well within the part's 3.8V to 32V spec. Want me to drop a load or a 3.3 V rail label on the output?
