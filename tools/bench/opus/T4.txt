Looking at your design, the regulator is not outputting 12 V:

    vout [power 9–18 V] is your input rail (PWR1 → REG1.vin) — that's the 9–18 V coming in.
    vout#2 [rail 5 V] is the regulator's output (REG1.vout → LED1.vin) — that's 5 V, not 12 V.

So REG1 is a buck converter stepping your 9–18 V input down to 5 V, and the LED is running off that 5 V rail.

As for why the LED is bright — that's worth a look at LED1's internal schematic. A 5 V rail driving an LED indicator needs a series current-limit resistor sized for 5 V. If the resistor inside LED1 is missing or sized too small (or sized for a lower voltage), the LED draws too much current and runs hot/bright. The power budget shows only ~0.02 A on that rail, which is a sane indicator current, so the brightness might just be a high-efficiency LED at a normal 15–20 mA.
